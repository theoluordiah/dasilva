✿ Questions ✿

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How long do custom orders take?
+
5–14 days depending on complexity. Amigurumi take about a week; bags and garments up to 2 weeks. I'll always give you a clear timeline before we begin so there are zero surprises.
Do you ship internationally?
+
Yes! Domestic (Nigeria) delivery takes 2–5 business days. International varies — usually 7–21 days with tracking provided. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout or discussed via DM.
Can I request a specific anime character or theme?
+
That's my absolute favourite kind of order. Send me a reference image and your favourite character or colour palette and we'll figure it out together. The more detail, the better!
What yarn do you use — is it safe for kids?
+
Premium soft cotton and hypoallergenic acrylic from trusted suppliers. For kids' amigurumi, I use non-toxic stuffing and properly secured safety eyes (suitable for ages 3+). I'll always flag any concerns.
How do I care for my crochet pieces?
+
Hand wash gently in cold water with mild soap. Lay flat to dry. Avoid machine washing unless specifically told it's safe. Properly cared-for pieces will last years and honestly just get better over time.
